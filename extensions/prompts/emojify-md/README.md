# Emojify

Emojify enhances any text by intelligently inserting contextually relevant emojis at natural breaks, making your notes more expressive and visually engaging — without altering a single word of the original content.

## Features

- **Context-aware emoji selection** — chooses emojis that match the tone and subject matter of your text
- **Natural placement** — inserts emojis only at logical pauses or transitions, not randomly mid-sentence
- **No emoji stacking** — enforces a rule that two emojis never appear side by side
- **Non-destructive** — your original text is returned completely unchanged; only emojis are added
- **Right-click ready** — available in the context menu for quick access on any selected text

## Usage

1. Select the text you want to emojify in your note
2. Right-click to open the context menu
3. Choose **Emojify** from the menu
4. The enhanced text — with emojis inserted — replaces your selection

## Examples

**Input:**
> Today was a great day. I finished my project, went for a run, and made a delicious dinner.

**Output:**
> Today was a great day. 🌟 I finished my project, went for a run, 🏃 and made a delicious dinner. 🍝

---

**Input:**
> Meeting notes: Q1 goals reviewed. Budget approved. Team morale is high.

**Output:**
> Meeting notes: 📋 Q1 goals reviewed. Budget approved. ✅ Team morale is high. 🙌
